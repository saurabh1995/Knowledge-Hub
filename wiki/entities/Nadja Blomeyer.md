**Co-first author on the lumbar spine biomechanics RNN paper (ABME 2023); experimental biomechanics researcher at RWTH Aachen.**

## Roles & contributions

- **Co-first author**: [[sources/lumbar-spine-biomechanics-rnn-abme-2023]] (ABME 2023) — equal contribution with Saurabh Balkrishna Tandale on LSTM-based lumbar spine (L4L5) prediction under cyclic loading

## Research context

Blomeyer's contribution to the ABME 2023 paper centres on the **experimental biomechanics** side: the in vitro cyclic loading testing of 6 human lumbar spinal segments (L4L5) over 18-hour sessions, across three motion directions (flexion-extension, axial rotation, lateral bending). This experimental dataset — 179 training sequences derived from 6 specimens — is the foundation on which Tandale's LSTM model was trained and validated.

The co-first authorship reflects a collaborative structure where biomechanical expertise (experimental protocol, tissue preparation, loading chamber, specimen sourcing) was contributed by Blomeyer and the neural network surrogate modelling was contributed by Tandale. The paper bridges experimental spine mechanics and data-driven RNN modelling.

**Affiliation:** Institute of General Mechanics (IAM) or Institute of Biomechanics, RWTH Aachen University.
