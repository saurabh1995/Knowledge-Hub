**Versatile co-author at RWTH Aachen appearing across the cell imaging, FEM mechanics, and FPGA acceleration threads — one of the few collaborators who spans multiple research directions in this wiki.**

## Roles & contributions

- Co-author: [[sources/cnn-tenogenic-differentiation-cmpb-2021]] (CMPB 2021) — CNN classification of BMSCs/tenocytes/chondrocytes; contributed to the deep learning implementation and evaluation
- Co-author: [[sources/fpga-bnn-viscoplastic-mrc-2025]] (MRC 2025) — Binary Neural Network on FPGA (PYNQ Z2) for viscoplastic Lemaitre–Chaboche FEM; contributed to the BNN architecture and/or FPGA deployment

## Research context

Gulakala's appearance in both a biomedical image classification paper (CMPB 2021) and a hardware-accelerated FEM paper (MRC 2025) suggests a profile combining machine learning implementation skills with knowledge of computational mechanics — consistent with the interdisciplinary environment of Stoffel's group at IAM/RWTH Aachen.

The gap between CMPB 2021 and MRC 2025 (4 years) spans Tandale's core SNN/neuromorphic research phase. Gulakala re-appears at the FPGA thread, indicating either ongoing collaboration or a shift toward hardware-oriented NN research.

**Affiliation:** Institute of General Mechanics (IAM), RWTH Aachen University.
