**Researcher at RWTH Aachen University specialising in neural network-enhanced finite element methods and neuromorphic computing for engineering mechanics.**

## Affiliation
- Institute of General Mechanics (IAM), RWTH Aachen University, Aachen, Germany
- Email: tandale@iam.rwth-aachen.de
- Role: PostDoc

## Research focus
Tandale's work sits at the intersection of deep learning and computational solid mechanics. The central programme is replacing expensive classical FEM subroutines (material integration, stiffness computation) with trained neural networks that run faster and, in later work, consume less energy by exploiting neuromorphic hardware.

Four overlapping research threads:
1. **NN-enhanced FEM** — replacing constitutive laws and stiffness matrices with ANNs/RNNs using Sobolev training (2021–2022).
2. **Physics-based self-learning** — embedding physical constraints in RNN/SNN training so models self-update during simulation without labelled data (2022–2024).
3. **Neuromorphic / sustainable AI** — introducing third-generation Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) into FEM to achieve energy efficiency on chips such as Loihi and Xylo-Av2 (2023–2026).
4. **Cell imaging / mechanobiology** — applying CNNs to phase-contrast microscopy for non-invasive stem cell and chondrocyte characterisation (2020–2024); adjacent collaboration rather than core programme.
